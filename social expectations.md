To keep the party as safe and special as possible, we only ask a few things of our guests:

* **Please treat your fellow dancers, staff, and the equipment with love, care, and respect.** Unsafe and anti-social behaviour isn’t tolerated.

* **Please don't use your phone or talk loudly on the dance floor.** It's a sacred space to connect with the music, and there's plenty of space off the dance floor for phones and chats.

* **Try and put aside time for the entire party.** It's best experienced as a collective journey from start to finish. To this end, there are no pass-outs, and doors close at midnight.
